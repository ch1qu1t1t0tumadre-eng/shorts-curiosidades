**Descripción:** Two companies build towers in a windy city. One kid hangs a giant steel ball near the top, and his boss calls it dead weight. Then the storms come. The story is made up, but the physics is real: many supertall towers use a tuned mass damper, a huge pendulum that swings against the building's motion and reduces how much it sways in strong wind.
