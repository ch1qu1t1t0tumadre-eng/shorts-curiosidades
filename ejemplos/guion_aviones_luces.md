# Ejemplo de guion — "Why airlines dim the lights before landing"

**Ángulo:** lo que la aerolínea hace delante de ti y nunca explica + truco de seguridad práctico.

**Texto exacto para la voz (EN-US, ~41 s):**
Airlines turn off the lights before landing... and it's not so you can sleep. Stay till the end, because there's one thing you should do in the last ten minutes of every flight. Your eyes need minutes to adjust to the dark. Minutes you don't have. Every plane must get everyone out in ninety seconds, using half the exits. So the crew dims the cabin early. If the power dies, your eyes are already ready. That's also why the shades go up: they need to see fire before opening a door. So here's the move. Before landing, count the rows to your nearest exit. In smoke, you can't see signs. But you can feel seats. Tell me below if you knew this, and follow for what airlines never explain.
