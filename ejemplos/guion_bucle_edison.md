# Ejemplo FORMATO BUCLE — Edison's last breath (HISTORIA / CIENCIA)

**Ángulo:** protagonista famosísimo (Edison) + objeto absurdo y un poco macabro que existe de verdad (su "último aliento" en un tubo de ensayo, expuesto en un museo).

**Fuentes verificadas:** The Henry Ford (ficha del objeto 51.13.51.3: tubo de 1931, sellado con parafina, del dormitorio donde murió Edison; Charles Edison vio tubos abiertos en una gradilla junto a la cama, el médico los selló y Charles envió uno a Henry Ford, que lo guardó en su casa, Fair Lane; expuesto en el Henry Ford Museum, Dearborn, Michigan). Smithsonian (carta de Charles Edison de 1953: gradilla de 8 tubos vacíos junto a la cama). Lo de que Ford creía que el alma sale con el último aliento es leyenda → se dice como "Legend says".

**Texto exacto para la voz (EN-US, 132 palabras, 724 caracteres):**
[intrigued] Thomas Edison's last breath is sealed inside a glass tube in Michigan. And it's real. In 1931, Edison was dying in his bedroom. Next to his bed sat a rack of eight empty test tubes. When Edison died, his son Charles had the doctor seal those tubes with wax. Trapping the air of the room where Edison breathed his last. [dramatic] In other words, he bottled his father's final breath. Then he sent one tube to Edison's best friend, Henry Ford. Ford idolized Edison. Legend says Ford believed the soul leaves the body with the last breath. Ford kept that tube at his house until he died. Today it's still sealed, on display at the Henry Ford Museum. So next time someone says save your breath, remember: Henry Ford actually did. He saved
